## What a TPM does

The core of the job, and the concepts worth mastering:

- **Program governance** — steering committees, decision rights, operating cadence.
- **Dependency management** — mapping cross-team dependencies and protecting the critical path.
- **Risk management (RAID)** — Risks, Assumptions, Issues, and Dependencies, tracked and owned.
- **Stakeholder communication** — audience-tuned status, executive reporting, expectation-setting.
- **Delivery planning** — roadmaps, milestones, capacity, and realistic sequencing.
- **Launch & readiness** — go/no-go criteria, cutover planning, rollback.

## Frameworks & methods

- **Scrum** — the most common agile delivery framework. [scrum.org](https://www.scrum.org)
- **Kanban** — flow-based delivery and work-in-progress limits.
- **SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework)** — scaling agile across many teams. [scaledagileframework.com](https://www.scaledagileframework.com)
- **OKRs** — Objectives and Key Results for aligning goals to outcomes.
- **RACI** — clarifying who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed.
- **RICE** — prioritization by Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort.
- **WSJF (Weighted Shortest Job First)** — prioritization by cost of delay over job size.
- **Critical Path Method** — finding the sequence that determines your timeline.

## Metrics that matter

- **DORA metrics** — deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, time to restore. The standard for delivery health. [dora.dev](https://dora.dev)
- **Flow metrics** — throughput, cycle time, work-in-progress, flow efficiency.
- **Program health** — milestone variance, RAID burn-down, dependency aging.
- **Outcome metrics** — tie delivery to the business result, not just output.

## Tools of the trade

- **Jira** — issue tracking and agile boards. [atlassian.com/software/jira](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira)
- **Confluence** — documentation and knowledge base. [atlassian.com/software/confluence](https://www.atlassian.com/software/confluence)
- **Linear** — fast, modern issue tracking. [linear.app](https://linear.app)
- **Asana** — work and project management. [asana.com](https://asana.com)
- **Notion** — flexible docs, wikis, and lightweight tracking. [notion.so](https://www.notion.so)
- **Miro** — whiteboarding, dependency mapping, planning. [miro.com](https://miro.com)
- **Smartsheet** — spreadsheet-style program tracking. [smartsheet.com](https://www.smartsheet.com)
- **Tale** — open-source project workspace to assign tasks to teammates and AI agents, then review their deliverables together. [tale.dev](https://tale.dev)

## Books

The shelf worth reading, roughly in order of usefulness to a TPM:

- **Making Things Happen** — Scott Berkun. The classic on the realities of running projects.
- **An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management** — Will Larson.
- **Project to Product** — Mik Kersten. Shifting from project thinking to product flow.
- **Team Topologies** — Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais. How team structure shapes delivery.
- **Accelerate** — Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim. The science of delivery performance.
- **The Phoenix Project** — Gene Kim et al. A novel that teaches flow and DevOps.
- **Inspired** — Marty Cagan. Product thinking every TPM should understand.
- **Escaping the Build Trap** — Melissa Perri. Outcomes over output.
- **The Manager's Path** — Camille Fournier. For the leadership side of the role.

## Certifications

- **PMP (Project Management Professional)** — the most recognized PM credential. [pmi.org](https://www.pmi.org)
- **PgMP (Program Management Professional)** — PMI's program-level credential.
- **PMI-ACP** — agile practitioner certification.
- **SAFe certifications** — for scaled agile environments. [scaledagile.com](https://scaledagile.com)
- **CSM (Certified ScrumMaster)** — entry point into Scrum facilitation.

## AI for TPMs

The fast-moving frontier — using AI to do the work, not just talk about it:

- **Claude** — strong reasoning and long-context work. [claude.com](https://claude.com)
- **GitHub Copilot** — AI assistance in the editor. [github.com/features/copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot)
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — the open standard for connecting AI to tools and data. [modelcontextprotocol.io](https://modelcontextprotocol.io)
- **Prompt engineering** — learning to get reliable, structured output from LLMs.
- **Responsible AI** — knowing when to trust AI output and when to verify it.
